I work on the problem of knowing where you are when GPS is unavailable, degraded, or can't be trusted. In practice that means inertial navigation on MEMS-grade sensors, recursive Bayesian estimation — particle filters, Kalman filters and their nonlinear variants — and map matching against the Earth's own geophysical signatures: gravity anomaly, magnetic anomaly, and bathymetry.

I'm finishing a Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering at Temple University on exactly that problem. Alongside it I build and maintain open-source navigation tooling in Rust and Python, publish through the Institute of Navigation, and teach graduate robotic navigation at WPI.
