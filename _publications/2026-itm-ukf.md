An unscented Kalman filter approach to navigation in GNSS-denied environments, using
geophysical anomaly maps — gravity, magnetic, and bathymetric — to bound the drift of a
strapdown inertial navigation system mechanized on MEMS-grade sensors. The UKF formulation is
the deterministic-sampling counterpart to the particle filter presented at Pacific PNT, and
the two are compared on the same trajectories.
