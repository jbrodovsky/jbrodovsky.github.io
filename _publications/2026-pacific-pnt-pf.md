A particle filter formulation for GNSS-denied navigation using MEMS-grade inertial sensors
aided by geophysical map matching. The filter treats gravity anomaly, magnetic anomaly, and
bathymetry as measurement sources against pre-existing global maps, and is evaluated on
trajectories from the accompanying open MEMS navigation dataset.
