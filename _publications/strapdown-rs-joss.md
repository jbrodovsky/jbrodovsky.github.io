A software paper describing [strapdown-rs]({{ site.url }}/portfolio/strapdown-rs), an
open-source Rust implementation of a strapdown inertial navigation system: a WGS84 Earth
model, the standard nine-state forward mechanization, UKF- and particle-filter-based
full-state INS implementations, and a command-line simulator for generating GNSS-degraded and
GNSS-denied scenarios.
