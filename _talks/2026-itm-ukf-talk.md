An unscented Kalman filter approach to bounding inertial drift in GNSS-denied environments by
map matching against geophysical anomaly fields, mechanized on MEMS-grade inertial sensors.
The talk covers the measurement model for gravity, magnetic, and bathymetric anomaly maps, the
sigma-point formulation, and a comparison against the particle filter approach on identical
trajectories — including where the deterministic-sampling approach wins on cost and where it
fails on multi-modality.
