A particle filter for GNSS-denied navigation on MEMS-grade inertial sensors, aided by
geophysical map matching. The talk covers the filter formulation, the practical problems of
running a particle filter against coarse global anomaly maps, and evaluation on the open MEMS
navigation dataset.
