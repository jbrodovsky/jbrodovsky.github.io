A graduate-level online asynchronous course on autonomous robotic navigation, developed and
taught in the Robotics Engineering Department at Worcester Polytechnic Institute.

The course runs from basic Bayesian filtering up through marine-grade inertial navigation:
recursive state estimation and the Kalman filter, its nonlinear extensions and the particle
filter, then strapdown INS mechanization, the reference frames it lives in, IMU error modeling
and error budgeting, and GNSS/INS integration — closing with what to do when GNSS isn't
available.

The emphasis throughout is on navigation as an engineering discipline rather than a collection
of filters: error budgets, observability, sensor selection, and knowing which assumptions your
estimator is quietly making about the world.
