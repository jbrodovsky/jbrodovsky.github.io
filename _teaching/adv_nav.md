A graduate special-topics course on advanced robotic navigation at Worcester Polytechnic
Institute.

The course works from inertial sensing up to full navigation systems: strapdown inertial
navigation mechanization and the reference frames it lives in, IMU error modeling and
allocation of an error budget, GNSS fundamentals and GNSS/INS integration, and recursive
Bayesian estimation — Kalman filtering and its nonlinear extensions, then particle filtering.
The back half covers what to do when GNSS is unavailable: terrain- and map-relative
navigation, geophysical map matching, and visual-inertial odometry.

The emphasis throughout is on navigation as an engineering discipline rather than a
collection of filters — error budgets, observability, sensor selection, and knowing which
assumptions your estimator is quietly making about the world.
